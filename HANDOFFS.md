# Prepared handoffs

These records describe completed preparation and required outside inputs. They do not send a message, appoint an author or authorize a submission. Routine drafting and verification remain authorized.

## H-001 — Review prepared conference materials

- **HANDOFF ID:** H-001
- **TASK:** CW-005 and CW-006
- **PROJECT:** Prepared candidate manuscripts and presentations in the restricted conference index.
- **FROM:** Conference execution work
- **TO:** Responsible authors and presenting author; an independent domain reviewer where the project requires one.
- **WHAT IS READY:** Canonical draft PRs with prepared documents, executable evidence, claim limits and route-specific submission requirements.
- **WHAT IS REQUIRED:** Review the actual materials; confirm contributor list/order, affiliations and contact details, accountability/AI-use disclosure, presenter eligibility and attendance, and compatibility with existing submissions. Reconcile any explicitly unverified current template before upload.
- **FILES/LINKS:** [Pinned project and route index](https://github.com/THE-BU1LD/org-infra-/blob/d732011254d071e2d2d3b1bb5866187c16c30dba/conferences/index.json); follow each canonical project link.
- **DEADLINE:** The selected route's recorded official cutoff; source conflicts and unknown timezones must be resolved before acting.
- **STATUS:** WAITING_EXTERNAL

## H-002 — Supply authentic source or study input

- **HANDOFF ID:** H-002
- **TASK:** CW-003 and CW-004
- **PROJECT:** The specific source/data/study-definition-dependent records in the restricted index.
- **FROM:** Completed recovery and verification work
- **TO:** Canonical source/artifact custodian or responsible study lead.
- **WHAT IS READY:** Exact known artifact identities, unsuccessful recovery attempts, retained evidence boundaries and explicit intake/resume requirements.
- **WHAT IS REQUIRED:** A distinct accessible authentic source/data package or definitive canonical-source routing; permissible data and frozen evaluation inputs where specified. An unrelated historical study or replacement synthetic result does not satisfy the dependency.
- **FILES/LINKS:** [Pinned source dependencies](https://github.com/THE-BU1LD/org-infra-/blob/d732011254d071e2d2d3b1bb5866187c16c30dba/conferences/index.json) and [blocker records](BLOCKERS.md).
- **DEADLINE:** Before the dependent research or submission can be completed; no invented recovery date.
- **STATUS:** BLOCKED
